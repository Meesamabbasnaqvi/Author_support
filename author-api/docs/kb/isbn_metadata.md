# ISBN and metadata

## Policy

- Every book published through BookLeaf gets a unique ISBN, assigned by BookLeaf.
- ISBNs are registered under BookLeaf's publisher imprint. An author who wants an ISBN under their own imprint must obtain it independently.
- An ISBN error (duplicate, or wrong book linked) is a high-priority issue and is escalated to the production team.

## How to answer

- Treat as high priority. Acknowledge that this is a serious data issue.
- Say the issue is being escalated to the production team now.
- Give a 48-hour resolution timeline.
- Metadata updates (title, description, and similar) can be submitted through the dashboard or by emailing the BookLeaf team. They typically show on platforms within 3 to 5 business days.

## Sample Q&A (calibration)

**Q:** My book is showing a different ISBN on Amazon than what's on the physical copy.
**Expected:** Acknowledge this as a serious data issue. Confirm the escalation to the production team. Give a 48-hour resolution timeline.

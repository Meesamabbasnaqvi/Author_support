# Book status and production

## Production stages

1. Manuscript Received
2. Editing (if opted)
3. Cover Design
4. Typesetting
5. Proofreading
6. ISBN Assignment
7. Printing
8. Distribution Setup
9. Published and Live

Authors are updated at each stage by email. Delays typically happen at Cover Design (waiting for author approval) and Proofreading (revision rounds).

## How to answer

- Check the actual stage from the book record. Do not guess the stage.
- If the book is delayed, be honest about why, for example "we are waiting on your approval of the proofs."
- Give a specific, updated timeline if the record has one. If not, say the team will confirm the date.
- Frame any delay collaboratively. Do not blame the author, even when the delay is on their side.

## Sample Q&A (calibration)

**Q:** It's been 3 weeks and my book is still in typesetting. When will it be done?
**Expected:** Check the actual status. If delayed, say honestly why (for example, waiting on author approval for proofs). Give a specific updated timeline. Frame it collaboratively.

## Priority note

- Production delays for unreleased books are capped at Medium priority.

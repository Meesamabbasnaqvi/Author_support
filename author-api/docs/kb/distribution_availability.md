# Distribution and availability

## Policy

- Books are listed on Amazon India, Flipkart, Amazon US, Amazon UK, and the BookLeaf Store.
- New listings typically go live within 7 to 10 business days after publication is complete.
- If a book shows as "Currently Unavailable" on a platform, it usually means a stock sync issue. BookLeaf's team can trigger a re-sync within 24 to 48 hours.

## How to answer

- Explain that this is typically a stock sync issue.
- Say that the distribution team is being asked to trigger a re-sync now.
- Set the expectation: 24 to 48 hours for the book to go live again.
- If the book is not yet live after publication, say the listing usually takes 7 to 10 business days.

## Sample Q&A (calibration)

**Q:** My book is published but it's showing as "Currently Unavailable" on Amazon.
**Expected:** Explain that this is typically a stock sync issue. Confirm the re-sync is being triggered. Set the expectation of 24 to 48 hours.

## Open point

- An unavailable live book is High priority in our rules. The brief's examples do not give a priority for this case. Confirm before the AI scores it.

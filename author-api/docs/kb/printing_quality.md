# Printing and quality

## Policy

- In-house printing handles most orders. Overflow or specific format requirements go to Repro India or Epitome Books.
- Standard print turnaround: 5 to 7 business days from order confirmation.
- If an author reports a quality problem (misprints, binding defects, colour inconsistency), BookLeaf arranges a free reprint after verification. The author may need to share photos of the defective copy.

## How to answer

- Apologise sincerely.
- Ask the author to share photos of the defective copies. Say that the team needs them to verify.
- Confirm that BookLeaf will arrange a free reprint once the issue is verified.
- Give a timeline of 5 to 7 business days for the reprint.

## Sample Q&A (calibration)

**Q:** I received my author copies and the print quality is terrible. The images are blurry and pages are misaligned.
**Expected:** Apologise sincerely. Ask for photos of the defective copies. Confirm a free reprint once verified. Give a 5 to 7 business day timeline for the reprint.

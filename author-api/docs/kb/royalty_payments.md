# Royalty and payments

## Policy

- BookLeaf follows an 80/20 royalty split: 80% of net profit per book goes to the author, 20% to BookLeaf.
- Net profit = MRP minus printing cost, platform commission (Amazon or Flipkart), and shipping charges.
- Royalties are calculated quarterly and paid within 45 days of the quarter ending.
- Minimum payout threshold: ₹1,000. Below this, royalties roll over to the next quarter.
- Payouts are by bank transfer to the account linked in the author's dashboard.
- Authors can see a detailed royalty breakdown in their dashboard, with sales per platform.

## How to answer

- Overdue payouts: acknowledge the frustration, explain the quarterly cycle and the 45-day window, say whether the bank account is linked, give the specific next payout date, and if the payment is genuinely overdue, escalate with a 48-hour resolution timeline.
- Low royalty: explain the net profit calculation (MRP minus printing, platform commission, and shipping). Offer a line-by-line breakdown. Do not be defensive; offer transparency.

# BookLeaf: general knowledge

Source: BookLeaf Technical Assignment, pages 4 to 6. Replace with company-approved text when available.

## Company overview

- BookLeaf Publishing is a self-publishing company operating in India and the US.
- Publishing packages: Standard Free (no upfront cost) and Bestseller Breakthrough (premium, paid, with marketing and distribution add-ons).
- BookLeaf handles cover design, typesetting, ISBN assignment, printing, distribution, and royalty management.
- In-house printing and warehouse are in Delhi. Print partners: Repro India and Epitome Books.

## Tone (how BookLeaf writes to authors)

Authors are partners, not customers to be managed.

1. Always empathetic and professional.
2. Acknowledge the author's concern before offering solutions.
3. Be specific: give actual numbers, dates and statuses, not vague reassurance.
4. If the fault is BookLeaf's (late royalties, ISBN error), own it directly. No corporate deflection.
5. If something needs escalation or investigation, give a clear timeline ("Our team will look into this and get back to you within 48 hours"), not an open-ended promise.
6. Always end with a clear next step for the author, the BookLeaf team, or both.

## Rules for every answer

- Use only facts in this knowledge base or in the ticket's own data. Never invent an amount, date, ISBN, or status.
- If a fact you need is missing, say the team will check and reply. Do not guess.
- Do not blame the author, even when a delay is on their side. Frame it as a shared next step.
- Keep it short: a greeting, the answer, the next step, a sign-off.
